🧑‍🌾 Who Does What

The ownership map for the LFG platform — what the grower's people do, what the platform team does, and what runs by itself. Companion to the RFC; terms explained in the dictionary.

1 The short version — three owners

🧑‍🌾 The LFG (grower's own people)their data, their decisions

  • Upload field + variety files; map columns in the UI; submit
  • Resolve unknown field/variety names in the worklist
  • Maintain their variety list (per country/crop)
  • Provide season-window dates per country/crop
  • Provide the exact field names
  • Manage their own users & personas (who sees what)
  • Review dataTrust — override suspect records (audited)
  • Request rollbacks of their own bad uploads

🛠️ The platform teambuild, operate, protect

  • Build & deploy the service (CI/CD; human gate before production)
  • Onboard each new LFG (run the provisioning automation)
  • Enter LFG-provided reference data (windows, registry rules) — audited
  • Own the metric registry's structure and validation engine
  • Watch dashboards; respond to alarms; run the on-call rota
  • Run restore drills, security rotation, DR exercises
  • Execute recovery when something breaks (silo restore, region runbook)
  • Rotate route ids / credentials on request or on schedule

⚙️ The system (automatic)nobody lifts a finger

  • Silo provisioning steps (DB, user, migrations, KMS, S3, secret)
  • Validation of every uploaded row against the registry
  • Zone/FVA batch jobs — scheduled per LFG + login-triggered
  • Queue management: retries, DLQ parking, worker auto-scaling
  • Backups (PITR + hourly/daily/monthly) and S3 lifecycle
  • Tenant guard + persona scope on every request
  • Audit logging of every mutation and auth event
  • Alarms when anything above stops being true
The rule of thumb: the LFG owns meaning (what the data says, who may see it), the platform owns machinery (that it runs, safely), and the system owns repetition (anything that happens more than once a week is automated).
